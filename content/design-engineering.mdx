Design engineering is a discipline that's gaining recognition, and for good reason. It's the practice of bridging the gap between design and engineering — not as a translator, but as a practitioner who operates fluently in both worlds.

## What Design Engineering Is

A design engineer:

- Writes production code (React, TypeScript, CSS)
- Makes design decisions (layout, typography, color, motion)
- Thinks about product (user flows, edge cases, accessibility)
- Cares about performance (Core Web Vitals, bundle size)

It's not "design-aware developer" or "coding designer." It's a distinct role that owns the implementation of the design system and the quality of the user experience.

## The Tools

The modern design engineering stack includes:

- **Tailwind CSS** for styling
- **Framer Motion** for animations
- **Radix UI** for accessible primitives
- **shadcn/ui** for component patterns
- **Storybook** for component documentation

## Why It Matters

Companies are realizing that the handoff model — designer creates, developer implements — is inefficient. Design engineers eliminate the handoff entirely.
